Late October · Montréal & Toronto

What to pack

Think cool, damp autumn — not deep winter. You will walk a lot, sit in restaurants, stand in Niagara mist, and spend one morning on a spa boat. Tick the boxes as you pack; they stay on this device.

Montréal · 20–23 Oct

5–11°Ctypical late-October range
rainoften 8–11 wet days in the last third of the month

Cooler than Toronto, windier on Mount Royal. October is also one of Montréal’s wetter months. A real waterproof shell matters more than a heavy coat.

Toronto & Niagara · 23–28 Oct

7–12°Ca little milder than Montréal
mistNiagara boat + Behind the Falls = wet

Lake wind on the islands ferry can feel several degrees colder. Pack one extra warm layer for Niagara and the ferry even if the city feels fine.

Clothes for everyone

The formula is layers: a breathable base, a warm mid-layer, and a waterproof shell you can zip over everything. You do not need a full winter parka unless a cold snap is forecast the week you leave.

Prescriptions & health

Canada allows visitors to bring a personal supply — generally a 90-day course — in original packaging. Keep medicines in your carry-on, never in checked bags.

Include the generic (INN) name in Latin letters.

Motion sickness helps on the Niagara boat.

Controlled medicines (some sleep, pain, or ADHD treatments) have tighter rules — usually a 30-day supply, original packaging, and you must declare them if asked. If anyone takes these, get a doctor’s letter before you fly.
