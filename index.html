The rhythm

Days 1–3 in Montréal. A midday train on Friday. Toronto is slower: one concert, one garden day, Niagara, then the islands before flying home.

Arrive and rest

Arrival

Land at Pierre Elliott Trudeau, clear immigration, then go straight to the lodging. Keep dinner nearby and light after the long flight.

Wed 21 Oct · Day 2

Markets, Old Montréal, AURA

Montréal

Morning

Marché Jean-Talon

One of North America’s great public markets, in Little Italy. Walk the stalls for fruit, cheese, maple, and a slow coffee. Atwater Market is the backup if you prefer the Lachine Canal side of town.

Jean-Talon Atwater
Afternoon

Le Vieux-Montréal

A gentle look at the old city: stone streets, the port, and a café stop rather than a long walk. Stay close to Notre-Dame so the evening show is easy.

mtl.org
18:00

AURA at Notre-Dame Basilica

A 20-minute immersive light-and-music piece by Moment Factory inside the basilica. Sit, look up, and let the building become the stage. Tickets are evening-only online via Fever.

AURA Tickets
Dinner

9TailFox

Modern Korean cooking from Seoul-born chefs, in a small room on Notre-Dame Ouest. Reserve: it is a favourite and not a large restaurant.

9tailfox.ca

Thu 22 Oct · Day 3

Spa boat and maple lookout

Montréal

Morning · only spa of the trip

Bota Bota, spa-sur-l'eau

A floating spa on an old ferry in the Old Port: saunas, cold plunges, hot baths, and river views. Bring a swimsuit, sandals, a reusable bottle, and a bag for the wet suit. Robes and towels are provided. Go to the boat, not the postal address.

botabota.ca
Afternoon

Belvédère Camillien-Houde

A Mount Royal lookout for city and fall colour. A short trail, not a hike. Wind is cooler up here — wear the jacket from the packing list.

montreal.ca Trail
Dinner

Bouillon Albert

Classic Paris-style bouillon on Saint-Denis: marrow, tartare, and generous plates in a lively room. Book ahead.

bouillonalbert.ca

Fri 23 Oct · Day 4

Train to Toronto

Montréal → Toronto

10:49 – 16:18

VIA Rail · Gare Centrale to Union Station

About 5 hours 30 minutes across the corridor. Be at Gare Centrale early with tickets on your phone. The train has Wi-Fi and a café car. Arrival is downtown Toronto.

VIA Rail
Dinner

156 OneFiveSix

Contemporary Korean on Queen West. Intimate room, open kitchen, Tuesday–Sunday from 17:00, closed Monday. Settle in after the train, then a first Toronto dinner.

156onefivesix.com

Sat 24 Oct · Day 5

Distillery and candlelight

Toronto

Early evening

Distillery Historic District

Victorian brick distillery buildings, now pedestrian lanes, galleries, and cafés. Easy to wander before dinner. Wear comfortable shoes: cobblestones.

thedistillerydistrict.com
Dinner

Madrina Bar y Tapas

Catalan tapas in the Distillery District. Share plates, gin and tonics, and a Michelin Plate kitchen. Book so you are not rushed before the concert.

madrinatapas.com
20:45

Candlelight: Tribute to ABBA

String tribute under candlelight at Metropolitan Community Church, 115 Simpson Avenue — not in the Distillery. About 60 minutes; doors open 45 minutes before. Plan a taxi or ride from dinner.

Tickets

Sun 25 Oct · Day 6

Garden and a quiet evening

Toronto

Daytime

Toronto Botanical Garden

Outdoor gardens plus a glasshouse beside Edwards Gardens. Late October is quieter; the indoor rooms help if it rains. Home-cooked dinner after.

torontobotanicalgarden.ca

Mon 26 Oct · Day 7

Niagara Falls day tour

Niagara

Full day

Niagara Falls

A Paran Tours day trip from Toronto: Table Rock views, optional Hornblower cruise, Niagara-on-the-Lake, and a winery stop. It will be wet and colder than the city — waterproof jacket, and shoes that can take mist. Pickup 7:30 at their North York office. Home dinner after.

Paran Tours

Tue 27 Oct · Day 8

Toronto Islands

Toronto

Morning / afternoon

Toronto Islands ferry

Ferry from Jack Layton Terminal at 9 Queens Quay West. Walk the shore paths for the skyline. Ferries run all year; dress for wind on the water. Evening meal at home.

City ferry info
